# Logging and Monitoring Policy

- Security-relevant events (sign-ins, privilege changes, admin actions, access to Restricted data) are logged.
- Logs are sent to a central service, protected from tampering, and kept for at least 12 months.
- Alerts for high-risk events are reviewed promptly; log reviews are recorded.
- System clocks are synchronised to a trusted time source.
