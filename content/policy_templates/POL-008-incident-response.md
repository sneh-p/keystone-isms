# Incident Response Policy

## Reporting
Everyone reports suspected security events immediately to {{isms_owner}} or the IT helpdesk. When in doubt, report: there is no blame for raising a false alarm.

## Response steps
1. **Triage:** confirm whether it is an incident, assign an owner and a severity.
2. **Contain:** stop the spread, for example by isolating devices or disabling accounts.
3. **Eradicate:** remove the cause.
4. **Recover:** restore systems and confirm normal operation.
5. **Learn:** record the root cause and lessons learned, and fix weaknesses.

## Notification
If personal data is involved, {{isms_owner}} assesses whether regulators and affected people must be notified, and does so within the legal deadline (72 hours under GDPR). Customers are notified as contracts require.

## Evidence
Actions and timings are recorded in the incident timeline. Evidence is preserved so that it can be relied on later.

## Exercises
The response process is tested at least once a year with a tabletop exercise.
