# Information Security Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets out how {{company}} protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the information it holds and the systems it relies on. It is the top-level policy of the information security management system (ISMS); topic policies and procedures sit underneath it.

## Scope
{{scope}}

It applies to everyone who works for or on behalf of {{company}}: employees, contractors, temporary staff and third parties with access to our information or systems.

## Our commitments
Top management commits to:

- set measurable security objectives and review progress at least once a year;
- provide the people, time and budget needed to run the ISMS;
- identify and treat information security risks in line with our risk appetite;
- meet our legal, regulatory and contractual obligations;
- continually improve the ISMS based on audits, incidents and feedback.

## Responsibilities
- **Top management** owns this policy and approves risk acceptance above appetite.
- **{{isms_owner}}** runs the ISMS day to day and reports on it to top management.
- **Managers** make sure their teams follow security policies.
- **Everyone** follows this policy and the topic policies, completes security training, and reports suspected incidents straight away.

## Topic policies
This policy is supported by topic policies covering, among others: acceptable use, access control, asset management, data handling, cryptography, backup, incident response, business continuity, change management, supplier security and vulnerability management.

## Compliance
Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed and approved by {{isms_owner}}, and are time limited.

## Review
This policy is reviewed every {{review_months}} months, and after significant changes to the business, technology or threat landscape.
