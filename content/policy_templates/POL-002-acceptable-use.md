# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
Explains how people at {{company}} may use company information, devices, accounts and services.

## Rules everyone follows
1. Use company systems for business purposes. Limited personal use is fine if it does not affect work, security or the law.
2. Keep your accounts to yourself. Never share passwords or multi-factor authentication codes, and never use someone else's account.
3. Lock your screen when you step away, and keep desks clear of confidential paper.
4. Store company data only in approved company services, never in personal email, personal cloud storage or unapproved AI tools.
5. Install software only from approved sources, and keep devices updated.
6. Do not try to bypass security controls, test systems you are not authorised to test, or access data you do not need.
7. Be careful with links and attachments. If something looks wrong, report it instead of clicking.
8. When you work remotely, use trusted networks or the company VPN, and keep devices out of sight in public places.
9. Return all company equipment and data when you leave or change role.

## Monitoring
{{company}} monitors its systems for security and operational purposes, in line with applicable law.

## Reporting
Report lost devices, suspected phishing or any security concern to {{isms_owner}} or the IT helpdesk immediately.
