# Business Continuity Policy

- {{company}} identifies its critical services and sets recovery time and recovery point objectives for each.
- Continuity and ICT recovery plans describe how critical services continue or are restored during a disruption, including who decides and how people communicate.
- Security controls stay in place during a disruption, or are replaced by agreed temporary measures.
- Plans are tested at least once a year and updated after tests and real events.
