# Supplier Security Policy

- Suppliers are recorded in the vendor register and tiered by the data they handle and the access they have.
- Before engaging a supplier that handles Confidential data or connects to our systems, we assess its security (questionnaire, certifications, or both).
- Contracts include security, confidentiality, incident notification and data protection terms suited to the supplier's tier. A data processing agreement is signed when the supplier processes personal data.
- Critical suppliers are reassessed at least once a year; others according to their tier.
- When a relationship ends, access is removed and our data is returned or deleted.
