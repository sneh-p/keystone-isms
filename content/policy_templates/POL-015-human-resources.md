# Human Resources Security Policy

- Background checks proportionate to the role and allowed by law are completed before someone starts.
- Employment contracts and contractor agreements include confidentiality and security responsibilities.
- Everyone completes security awareness training when they join and at least once a year after that.
- Security breaches are handled through the disciplinary process.
- When someone leaves or changes role, their access is updated and they are reminded of continuing confidentiality duties.
