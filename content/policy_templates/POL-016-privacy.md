# Privacy and Data Protection Policy

- {{company}} processes personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently, only for specified purposes, and keeps no more than it needs.
- A record of processing activities is maintained.
- Privacy notices explain what we collect and why.
- Requests from people to access, correct or delete their data are handled within legal deadlines.
- Personal data breaches are handled under the Incident Response Policy, including notification where required.
- Personal data is shared with third parties only under a contract that protects it.
