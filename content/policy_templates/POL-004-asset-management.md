# Asset Management Policy

## Inventory
{{company}} keeps an inventory of its information assets: hardware, software, cloud services, data stores and key suppliers. Each asset has a named owner, a classification and a status. The inventory is reviewed at least every six months.

## Ownership
Asset owners decide who may access the asset, make sure it is protected according to its classification, and keep its inventory entry up to date.

## Endpoint devices
Company laptops and phones are enrolled in device management, encrypted, protected by a screen lock and kept up to date.

## Lifecycle
- New assets are added to the inventory before they go into use.
- Assets are retired through a controlled process, and storage is wiped or destroyed before disposal or re-use.
- Leavers return all company assets on their last day.
