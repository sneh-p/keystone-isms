# Data Classification and Handling Policy

## Classification levels
| Level | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Marketing material, published documents | No restrictions |
| **Internal** | Internal procedures, general business email | Company systems only |
| **Confidential** | Customer data, contracts, financial data | Need-to-know access, encrypted in transit and at rest |
| **Restricted** | Payment card data, health data, credentials, security reports | Named people only, extra logging, never sent by email |

## Rules
- Information owners classify the information they create. If in doubt, treat it as Confidential.
- Share Confidential and Restricted information only through approved, encrypted services, and only with people who need it.
- Keep information only as long as the retention schedule requires, then delete it securely.
- Do not copy Confidential or Restricted data to removable media or personal devices.
